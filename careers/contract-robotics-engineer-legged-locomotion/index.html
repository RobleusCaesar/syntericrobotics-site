Contract Robotics Engineer: Legged Locomotion

Synteric Robotics · Boulder/Denver, CO (remote-friendly) · Contract

About us

Synteric Robotics is a CU Boulder spinout building small, autonomous legged robots that go where people can’t: crawl spaces, attics and other tight, dirty spaces. Our six-legged prototype is walking, and restoration companies are lined up to field-test it. Now we need it to walk well.

The role

You’ll own the gait. You’ll take our hexapod from basic walking to a robust controller that handles real crawl-space terrain: uneven dirt, rocks, concrete footers and loose plastic sheeting. You’ll work alongside a CU Boulder robotics lab and a small engineering team handling sensors, mapping and the simulation model. Work is milestone-based, and we’ll define the milestones together.

What we’re looking for

  • You’ve built a walking controller and run it on a real legged robot, not just in simulation. It can be learned or model-based.
  • You’re comfortable in a physics simulator (MuJoCo, Isaac, PyBullet or similar) and with Python.

Nice to have

  • Reinforcement learning for locomotion
  • Experience getting simulation results to hold up on hardware
  • Multi-legged robots (quadruped counts)
  • ROS 2
  • Hobby- or servo-grade actuators like Dynamixel

Wish list

  • Hexapod experience
  • Rough-terrain or “blind” (no-camera) walking
  • Deploying policies on NVIDIA Jetson or other edge hardware
  • Published papers, open-source repos or robot videos we can look at
  • Startup or contract experience
  • Based in Colorado

Details

  • Contract, 40 hours/week, starting November 2026
  • Grad students, postdocs and recent grads are welcome
  • Rate: $50–$90/hour, based on experience

To apply

Send a short note and a link to something you’ve built that walks (video, paper or repo) to rob@syntericrobotics.com.
